Part 1 · Bitcoin and the Monetary Role of Real Estate

Chapter 1

The Internet of Value

The appendix opens with behavior rather than price. HODLing marks the shift from trading bitcoin to saving in it.

“HODL” and the Emergence of Bitcoin as a Store of Value

The term “HODL” originated in 2013 on the Bitcointalk forum, when a user known as GameKyuubi posted “I AM HODLING” during a sharp price decline. What began as a misspelling became shorthand for long-term accumulation, prioritizing retention over tactical market timing.

Chapter 3

The Role of the Monetary Premium

A monetary premium is the value an asset carries beyond its use. Japan shows that premium can drain away over decades.

Japan: Real Estate, Monetary Conditions, and Demographic Change

Following the asset price bubble of the late 1980s, Japanese real estate values declined sharply and, in many regions, have not recovered to prior peaks even decades later.
