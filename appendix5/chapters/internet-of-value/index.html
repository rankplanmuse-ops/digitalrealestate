The Internet of Value

“HODL” and the Emergence of Bitcoin as a Store of Value

The term “HODL” originated in 2013 on the Bitcointalk forum, when a user known as GameKyuubi posted a message titled “I AM HODLING” during a sharp bitcoin price decline. In it, he described his decision to hold bitcoin rather than attempt to trade short-term market movements. What began as a misspelling quickly became shorthand for a broader investment behavior.

The term is sometimes interpreted as “Hold On for Dear Life,” reinforcing its association with maintaining exposure through periods of volatility. In practice, HODLing reflects a shift away from short-term trading toward long-term accumulation—consistent with bitcoin’s emerging role as a store of value.

This behavior prioritizes asset retention over tactical market timing. As bitcoin adoption has evolved, this mindset has played a role in shaping its use not merely as a medium of exchange, but as a monetary asset held for long-term purchasing power preservation.
