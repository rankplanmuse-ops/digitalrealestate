The Speculative Attack: The Case for Leveraged Bitcoin Exposure

Pierre Rochard's 2014 essay argued that rational actors would borrow fiat to acquire bitcoin. These sources revisit it.

Rochard's original thesis

The speculative attack describes a feedback loop: holders of weak money borrow it to buy hard money, accelerating the transition.
