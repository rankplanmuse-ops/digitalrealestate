About the author

Leon Wankum was one of the first financial economics students (MSc) to write a thesis on bitcoin in 2015. After graduating, he joined his family business, oneVest, which specializes in real estate investing, real estate development, and venture capital. From 2016 to 2020, he worked at RED baufeld GmbH, a real estate construction company, where he oversaw the construction of commercial and residential projects. His career led him to specialize in developing strategies that integrate bitcoin into real estate development, investment, and capital allocation. Leon began writing publicly about Bitcoin in 2020 and has been a longtime contributor to Bitcoin Magazine. Digital Real Estate is the culmination of more than a decade of experience in Bitcoin, real estate, investing, and finance, as well as his dedication to helping businesses and individuals leverage bitcoin’s potential.
