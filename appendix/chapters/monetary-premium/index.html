Part 1 · Bitcoin and the Monetary Role of Real Estate

Chapter 3: The Role of the Monetary Premium

A monetary premium is the value an asset carries beyond its use. Japan shows that premium can drain away over decades.

Japan: Real Estate, Monetary Conditions, and Demographic Change

Following the asset price bubble of the late 1980s, Japanese real estate values declined sharply and, in many regions, have not recovered to prior peaks even decades later.
