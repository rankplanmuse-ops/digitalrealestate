Extended Appendix

Extended Appendix

All sources across the book's five parts, with editorial notes on each one.

Chapters

Part 1 · Bitcoin and the Monetary Role of Real Estate

  • Chapter 1

    The Internet of Value

    The appendix opens with behavior rather than price. HODLing marks the shift from trading bitcoin to saving in it.

  • Chapter 2

    Why Real Estate Became the World's Primary Store of Value

    These conversations frame property as a savings technology people adopted by default, and ask what happens when a better one appears.

  • Chapter 3

    The Role of the Monetary Premium

    A monetary premium is the value an asset carries beyond its use. Japan shows that premium can drain away over decades.

  • Chapter 4

    Bitcoin vs. Real Estate

    A side-by-side look at liquidity, divisibility, carrying cost and seizure risk.

  • Chapter 5

    The Advantages of Real Estate

    Real estate still produces yield, shelter and utility. These sources argue for pairing the two rather than choosing.

Part 2 · Integrating Bitcoin into Real Estate Operations

  • Chapter 6

    Structuring for Bitcoin as a Strategic Asset

    Practical operations: accepting rent in bitcoin and holding reserves at the property-company level.

  • Chapter 7

    Bitcoin Mining as Real Estate Energy Infrastructure

    Mining turns stranded energy and land into a monetizable asset. Production cost also acts as a reference point for price.

Part 3 · Capital Structure and Real Estate Finance

  • Chapter 8

    Real Estate Refinancing: Using Leverage for Bitcoin Accumulation

    Equity locked in property can be refinanced and redirected. The sources explain the method and its risks.

  • Chapter 9

    Bitcoin as Collateral

    Lenders are beginning to accept bitcoin alongside or instead of property. Custody and verification decide who can lend.

  • Chapter 10

    Bitcoin in Development Finance

    How bitcoin-backed capital could fund construction where conventional credit is expensive or scarce.

Part 4 · Corporate Bitcoin Treasury Strategies

  • Chapter 11

    The Speculative Attack: The Case for Leveraged Bitcoin Exposure

    Pierre Rochard's 2014 essay argued that rational actors would borrow fiat to acquire bitcoin. These sources revisit it.

  • Chapter 12

    The Strategy Playbook

    Strategy (formerly MicroStrategy) turned the speculative attack into a capital-markets program.

  • Chapter 13

    The Metaplanet Story

    A Japanese hotel company reinvents itself as a bitcoin treasury, linking Chapter 3's Japan case to corporate strategy.

Part 5 · Monetary Transformation and the Future of Real Estate

  • Chapter 17

    Credit, Interest Rates, and Capital Formation

    Edward Chancellor's history of interest frames what happens when the price of time is suppressed.

  • Chapter 18

    The Built Environment Under Hard Money

    Low time preference changes what gets built. Bitcoin Urbanism imagines durable, beautiful places.

  • Chapter 20

    As the Monetary Foundation Changes, So Too Must the Business Models

    Closing sources on deflation, AI and abundance, led by Jeff Booth's The Price of Tomorrow.
